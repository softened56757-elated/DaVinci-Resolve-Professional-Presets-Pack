## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is this pack compatible with DaVinci Resolve 19?**
A: Yes, the DaVinci Resolve Professional Presets Pack is fully compatible with DaVinci Resolve 19 and later versions. It also works with the free version of DaVinci Resolve, though some features may require DaVinci Resolve Studio.

**Q: Can I use these presets commercially?**
A: Absolutely. All presets in this pack are royalty-free and can be used in both personal and commercial projects without any restrictions.

**Q: The presets don't appear after installation. What should I do?**
A: Ensure you've installed the presets to the correct directory. You can manually copy the preset files to `C:\ProgramData\Blackmagic Design\DaVinci Resolve\Support\Resolve Disk Database\Resolve Profiles\Users\Guest\Color Presets\` and restart DaVinci Resolve.

**Q: Do these presets work with other editing software?**
A: No, these presets are specifically designed for DaVinci Resolve. They are not compatible with Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or other NLEs.
